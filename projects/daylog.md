## A small place to keep your day

Write one entry for a date, with an optional title and tags. Keep mood, energy, focus, and notes alongside your reflection when those signals are useful to you.

## A gentle way back in

Open an optional writing prompt when the blank page feels difficult. Prompts offer a starting point without inserting text into your entry. Search your recorded entries or return through the month calendar.

## Look back at your rhythm

Check in on a habit and revisit its current and longest streaks. The calendar marks days with entries; neither a streak nor a rating measures the quality of a day. Daylog keeps this personal journal in a local SQLite store.
