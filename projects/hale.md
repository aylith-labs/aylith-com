hale is a personal, all-around health platform. Capture anything about your body — meals, drinks,
symptoms, measurements, meds, bathroom events — with a photo, a voice note, or a quick tap, and see
it all on one timeline. An LLM turns raw captures into structured entries; it's a mobile-first,
installable PWA. Multi-user-ready (invite-only) under the aylith umbrella.
