shefros is an Arch-based distribution where the desktop session **is** [shefrd](https://shefrd.dev):
every Super key is a shefrd action, every window is a pane, and a pane can hold a terminal, a
browser, or a game.

The argument is one level up from shefrd's own: shefrd dissolved the ceilings that came from being a
guest inside someone else's terminal. The ones that remain come from being a guest inside someone
else's desktop.
